Hotel & Lodge Management Software

Front desk software that keeps
every room in sync.

A real Front Desk Management System covering reservations, room board, housekeeping, maintenance, billing and expenditure — built for a working lodge, not a demo.

Front desk management software for hotels and lodges.

A hotel or lodge front desk needs reservations, room status, billing and housekeeping to stay in sync in real time — a booking made at the counter should immediately reflect on the room board. SURYA CODE builds Front Desk Management Systems (FDMS) that connect reservations, rooms, billing and operations into one dashboard.

How it works

Built as a web application (installable as a PWA, working offline with background sync) and, where a Windows desktop experience is preferred, as an Electron.js application with local-first storage and two-way Google Sheets sync — so the front desk keeps working even during a connectivity drop.

◆

Reservations & room board

Bookings, room status and a visual room board that stays in sync with the front desk.

◆

Check-in / check-out & folios

Guest check-in/out with folio creation and billing tied to the stay.

◆

Housekeeping & maintenance

Track room-cleaning status and maintenance issues alongside the room board.

◆

Billing & expenditure

Guest billing plus a separate expenditure module for day-to-day lodge expenses.

◆

Reports & dashboard

Date-range filtered reports (including daily room-register PDFs) and a dashboard overview.

◆

Role-based access

Admin-only actions such as room editing, with staff limited to day-to-day operations.
