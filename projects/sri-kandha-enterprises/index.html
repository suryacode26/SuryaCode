Case Study

Sri Kandha Enterprises
Billing & Inventory Software.

A GST-compliant billing and inventory management desktop application built for a retail/wholesale business, from tax invoicing to purchases and sales returns.

IndustryRetail / Wholesale
TypeWindows Desktop App
StackElectron.js, Vanilla JS
StatusDelivered, iterating

The problem

Sri Kandha Enterprises needed proper GST-compliant billing alongside inventory, purchases and sales-return tracking, in a desktop application that could print both quick thermal receipts and formal A4 tax invoices.

The solution

An Electron.js desktop billing and inventory management application with dedicated screens for POS billing, GST invoicing, reports, inventory, purchases and sales returns — plus counter-level printing controls tuned to how the business bills.

◆

GST tax invoicing

A4 GST-compliant tax invoices alongside 80mm/58mm thermal receipt formats.

◆

Original / Copy printing

An Auto Print Copy setting that prints Original then Copy automatically at billing time, plus a chooser on the Reports screen.

◆

Print-directly mode

A Skip Preview setting for counters that want printing to start immediately instead of opening a preview first.

◆

Inventory & purchases

Stock management alongside dedicated purchase-entry and sales-return screens.

◆

Reports & settings

A reports module and a settings screen controlling invoice formats and printing behaviour.

◆

Desktop-first reliability

Runs as an installed Windows application for consistent counter performance.

Technology

Electron.jsVanilla JavaScriptHTML/CSS

Development approach: built around GST invoicing requirements from the outset, then refined with printing-behaviour settings (Original/Copy, Skip Preview) based on how the counter actually wanted to print. No client revenue figures are published here.
