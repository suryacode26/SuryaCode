Case Study

Ananda Lodge
Front Desk Management System.

A real Front Desk Management System keeping reservations, the room board, housekeeping, maintenance and billing in sync for a working lodge.

IndustryHotel / Lodge
TypeWeb App (PWA) + Desktop Edition
StackVanilla JS, Bootstrap, Google Apps Script
StatusActive, iterating

The problem

Ananda Lodge needed reservations, room status, housekeeping, maintenance and billing to stay in sync at the front desk in real time, instead of living across a register book and separate memory.

The solution

A Front Desk Management System (FDMS) with a Google Sheets backend (via a full Google Apps Script API), available both as an installable web app (PWA, offline-capable) and as an Electron.js Windows desktop edition with local-first storage and two-way cloud sync — so the front desk keeps working through a connectivity drop either way.

◆

Reservations & room board

Bookings and a visual room board with an edit flow that reuses the reservation modal for quick updates.

◆

Housekeeping & maintenance

Dedicated modules tracking room-cleaning status and maintenance issues alongside daily operations.

◆

Billing & expenditure

Guest billing plus a full expenditure CRUD module for day-to-day lodge expenses.

◆

Daily room register reports

PDF reports generated per register slot, rooms listed in order, for daily front-desk record-keeping.

◆

Role-based room editing

Room-detail editing restricted to admin accounts; staff retain full add/remove/view access.

◆

Offline-first & dark mode

PWA offline support with a service worker, plus a full dark-mode theme with a persisted preference.

Technology

Vanilla JavaScriptBootstrapGoogle Apps ScriptGoogle SheetsElectron.js (desktop edition)Service Worker / PWA

Development approach: the backend (database, auth, settings and reservation logic) was reverse-engineered from the existing frontend API contract and then extended module by module — dashboard filters, expenditure tracking, pagination, dark mode and role-based permissions — with a separate Electron desktop edition kept in sync with the web edition's data model. No client revenue or occupancy figures are published here.
