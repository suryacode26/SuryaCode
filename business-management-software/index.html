Business Management Software

One dashboard, instead of five spreadsheets.

Attendance, staff roles, customer records, expenses and reporting brought into one connected system — built around your business, not a generic ERP template.

Replace scattered spreadsheets with one connected system.

Many small businesses run on a mix of notebooks, WhatsApp messages and separate spreadsheets for attendance, expenses and reports. Business management software from SURYA CODE brings that into one dashboard — without forcing the business to change how it already operates.

Modules that can be included

Attendance and staff management (including fingerprint-based attendance with offline-first sync), customer records, expense tracking, reporting and role-based dashboards. Systems are built modularly, so a business can start with what it needs now and add modules later.

◆

Fingerprint attendance

Check-in/out, breaks, late/overtime, leave and holiday tracking with fingerprint-device integration.

◆

Role-based dashboards

Admin, HR, manager and staff roles each see the data relevant to them.

◆

Customer records (CRM-lite)

A simple customer database tied to orders, billing or service history.

Expense & reports

Categorised expenses, filters, CSV/PDF export and printable reports.

Google Sheets sync

Data readable directly in Google Sheets alongside the application's own reports.

◆

Offline-first & backups

Local encrypted storage with scheduled backups, so operations never stop for a connectivity issue.
