Software Development

Custom software, built around your workflow.

Dashboards, business management tools and offline-capable Windows desktop applications, designed around how your business actually operates instead of a generic template.

Software planned around your workflow, not a generic template.

Off-the-shelf software forces a business to adapt to someone else's workflow. Custom software does the opposite — it is designed around the way your team actually works, so fewer workarounds and less manual double-entry are needed.

What gets built

Web-based systems on Java, Spring Boot and MySQL for businesses that need a hosted, multi-user application, and Electron.js desktop applications for Windows when a business needs an offline-capable, install-and-run system with local data storage and background sync to Google Sheets.

◆

Dashboards & reports

Business-overview dashboards, filterable reports and exportable data (CSV/PDF).

◆

Role-based access

Separate permissions for admin, staff and other roles so people only see what they need.

◆

Offline-first desktop apps

Electron.js Windows applications that keep working without internet, syncing later.

◆

Google Sheets integration

Two-way sync with Google Sheets/Apps Script for businesses that already work from spreadsheets.

◆

Attendance & HR modules

Fingerprint-based attendance, leave management and staff records for staff-heavy operations.

◆

Data backup & recovery

Local encrypted storage plus scheduled backups so business data is never dependent on one device.
